Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/07/2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Fernas ("Fernas," "we," "us," or "our") collects and uses personal data when you use fernasteam.com (the "Website"). Fernas is a student-founded venture studio based in Dhahran, Saudi Arabia.

Fernas is the "controller" of your personal data, meaning we decide how and why it is used. We handle personal data under the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its Implementing Regulations.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at contact@fernasteam.com.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

2. What we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum.

a) Information you give us through the contact form:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your phone number;
  • your idea or message, and anything you choose to include in it.

b) Information you send us by email. If you email us directly, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach.

c) Technical information collected by our hosting provider. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub automatically processes your IP address and basic request details (such as browser type and the time of your visit) to deliver the Website and keep it secure. Fernas does not receive these logs or use them to identify you.

d) A preference stored on your device. The Team page has a sound on/off button. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the page remembers it. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

What we don't collect:

  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • We do not load fonts or scripts from third-party servers.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal data, such as health, religious, financial, or biometric data. Please don't include any in your Submission.

3. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

We use the information you send us only to:

  • read and evaluate your idea or message;
  • reply to you and talk about your idea;
  • prepare a project agreement with you, if we decide to take your idea on;
  • keep a record of ideas we have received, so we can show where an idea came from if a question ever comes up later;
  • protect the Website and our inbox from spam and abuse.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give when you submit the form or email us. Where the law allows, we also rely on our legitimate interest in running the Website securely and keeping records of Submissions.

We do not use your data for marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

4. Sharing your data

We share personal data only with:

  • Fernas team members who need to review your Submission;
  • the service providers in Section 5, who process data on our behalf so the Website and form can work;
  • authorities, where Saudi law requires it, or where needed to protect our rights or someone's safety.

If we take on your idea, any further sharing (for example with funders or partners) will be set out in your project agreement and will happen only with your agreement.

5. Service providers we use

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): receives what you type into the contact form and forwards it to our email inbox.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts the Website and processes the technical information described in Section 2(c).

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy and security practices. We choose providers that protect personal data appropriately, and we give them only what they need.

6. Transfers outside Saudi Arabia

Some of the providers above store or process data outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. When your data is transferred abroad, we do so in line with the PDPL and SDAIA's Regulation on Personal Data Transfer outside the Kingdom. We use the safeguards that regulation requires, such as approved standard contractual clauses where applicable, and we transfer only the minimum data needed.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Ideas and messages we don't take on: deleted within 12 months of when we receive them.
  • Ideas we take on: kept for as long as the project agreement lasts, plus any period the law requires afterwards.
  • Email correspondence: kept only as long as the related Submission.

We may keep a minimal record (your name, the date, and a short description of the idea) for longer, to show where an idea came from. You can ask us to delete it as described in Section 9.

8. How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data:

  • The Website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Only team members who need access to Submissions have it.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach is likely to harm you, we will notify the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA) within the time the law requires (72 hours of becoming aware of it). We will also notify you without delay where the law requires it.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is collected and used (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • obtain a copy of it in a clear, readable format;
  • correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date;
  • have your data destroyed when we no longer need it, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did with your data before you withdrew.

To use any of these rights, email contact@fernasteam.com from the address you used to contact us, or tell us enough to identify your Submission. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will respond within the time the PDPL and its regulations allow (normally within 30 days). Making a request is free.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can file a complaint with the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

10. People under 18

The Website is intended for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please submit an idea only with the permission and involvement of a parent or legal guardian. If we learn that we have received personal data from someone under 18 without that permission, we will delete it.

11. Links to other websites

The Website links to other sites, including LinkedIn, Scalendars, and team members' personal portfolios. Once you leave fernasteam.com, the other site's privacy policy applies, not this one.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use data you have already sent us, we will tell you by email before it takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about your personal data can be sent to contact@fernasteam.com.
