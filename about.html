Our name

Where the name comes from.

فرناس .. للناس

Abbas ibn Fernas built wings and jumped, centuries before anyone had the vocabulary for it. He did it because the problem was in front of him and nobody else was going to solve it. He was a poet and an astronomer in ninth-century Córdoba, and he spent his life turning curiosity into objects that worked: a water clock, a planetarium of glass, a way of cutting rock crystal that his city had been importing at great cost. The flight is the part people remember, but the habit behind it is the part worth copying.

We took the name because the instinct is the same. A student sees a problem and has an idea for fixing it, and too often that is where it ends. Those ideas are our runway. We take the idea, put a team on it, fund it, and build it into something real. Then we move on to the next one.

Fernas is a venture studio founded by students. Bringing us an idea is free, and so is getting started: our teams do the building, and we cover the cost of the projects we take on.

Abbas ibn Fernas got the landing wrong. He wrote about it afterwards, worked out that he had built no tail, and left the note for whoever came next. That is the standard we hold ourselves to: publish the thing, say plainly what it does not do yet, and leave it in a state somebody else can pick up.

Principles

How we work.

  1. 01

    Free to start

    Bringing us an idea costs nothing, and neither does getting started.

  2. 02

    Shipped, not pitched

    A working link beats a slide deck. We release small and improve in public.

  3. 03

    Evidence over opinion

    We research the problem before we build the fix, and we publish what we find.

  4. 04

    Handed forward

    Members graduate. The projects stay, documented, for whoever picks them up next.
